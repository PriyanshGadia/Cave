# 1. THE STORY

The visitor begins in almost complete darkness.

A massive industrial metal door is directly ahead.

The door opens.

Behind it is a large futuristic workshop.

The workshop is initially almost completely dark.

A distant workbench is visible only as a silhouette.

On the workbench are multiple separated pieces of the VAULT MK-I armor.

At first the visitor cannot tell exactly what they are.

Then one object activates.

It is the helmet.

Two narrow rectangular optic apertures suddenly illuminate with restrained neon cyan.

That first activation should reveal enough geometry to identify it as a sophisticated helmet.

Then the other armor components sequentially wake.

The workshop gradually illuminates.

Now the visitor can clearly see:

```text
BOOTS
LEGS
TORSO / CHEST
ARMS
GAUNTLETS
HELMET
```

These are not assembled.

They are individual pieces arranged across the distant workbench.

Then the machine comes alive.

Each component contains its own propulsion architecture.

They lift from the workbench.

They accelerate toward the ceiling.

A large overhead hatch opens.

The components blast through it.

The camera follows.
