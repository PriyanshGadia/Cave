1. Subject & Scene Layout
Core Subject: A central vertical biometric security panel mounted on a close up massive, heavy-duty industrial sci-fi blast door embedded in cave entrance.

Environment: Built directly into a dark, rough-textured subterranean rock/cave ceiling and surrounding arch.

Central Control Terminal:

Top LED: A horizontal strip light glowing cool white/cyan.

Screen Display: A dark digital screen showing a cyan 3D point-cloud wireframe facial recognition scan, alongside UI elements like "SCANNING", "SCAN", and "BIOMETRIC SYSTEM ALPHA".

Iris Scanner: A prominent circular camera lens inside a metallic housing positioned immediately below the display screen.

Interactive Interface: A bottom row of backlit control buttons with text labels reading REGISTER, VERIFY, and RESET alongside small icon symbols (fingerprint, printer/card).

2. Surface Textures & Detail Work
Materials: Dark gunmetal grey steel, brushed titanium, matte black composite casing, and weathered iron plating.

Greebles & Mechanical Details: Exposed hex bolts, heavy corner rivets, recessed panels, hydraulic tubes/conduits running along the left side, and heatsink cooling vents.

Graphics & Graphics Decals: Crisp white stencil lettering ("RESTRICED ACCESS"), warning triangles, and subtle printed circuit trace line graphics.

Status Indicators: Dotted with tiny glowing green LED lights positioned strategically across the metal paneling.

Wear & Tear: Realistic micro-scratches on metal edges, subtle grime accumulating in recessed seams, and slight surface texture variations.

3. Lighting & Color Palette
Lighting Style: High-contrast dual-tone cinematic lighting (Chiaroscuro).

Primary Key Light: Warm amber/golden side-lighting originating from the upper left, casting rich highlights on the rough cavern ceiling and left panel edges while throwing deep shadows across the right side.

Secondary Accent Light: Cool cyan/teal electronic backlighting emanating from the central UI screen, status bar, and camera housing.

Tone: Dark, atmospheric, claustrophobic, and grounded high-tech sci-fi.

4. Camera & Rendering Technique
Composition: Perfectly centered, eye-level, straight-on orthographic/architectural perspective. Very close to the door, surrounding environment is not visible properly.

Framing & Aspect Ratio: Vertical framing (approx. 3:4 or 9:16 aspect ratio).

Render Quality: Crisp, photorealistic hard-surface 3D render (Unreal Engine 5 / Octane Render aesthetic) with high dynamic range (HDR) light falloff.
