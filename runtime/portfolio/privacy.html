Privacy & cookies.

Last updated 15 November 2026. This is Priyansh Gadia’s personal portfolio. You do not need an account to read it.

Browsing the portfolio

This 2D page does not include advertising, marketing pixels or third-party analytics. Its visuals and fonts are served locally. Essential session storage remembers your page position and, if you reserve a meeting, the reference needed to manage that reservation. No non-essential cookies are introduced by this page.

Arranging a meeting

Booking requires Google sign-in. With your consent, the scheduling service verifies the Google sign-in token and sends the email on the signed-in account, the selected time, and any optional note to Google Calendar for the invitation. The portfolio does not keep the sign-in token in browser storage. The public schedule shows bookable times and anonymous busy intervals. Private event names, descriptions and attendees are never published. Your own saved reservation references are visible only in your browser session. The backend keeps slot protection records and hashes for request throttling; it does not store your meeting note, name or email in its local slot table.

Slot protection records for meetings that ended over 45 days ago are removed when successful bookings are processed. To enforce booking limits, this browser saves a random identifier. The scheduling backend stores hashed account and browser identifiers with a count of 30-minute blocks reserved. The quota records are pruned after their booking day and the following UTC day as bookings are processed. Expired throttle records are pruned as the API is used. Google’s handling and retention of calendar events and invitations are governed by its own policies and your account settings.

Your choices

You can cancel a confirmed meeting using its management control in this browser. Clearing browser storage removes the locally saved management references, but does not cancel meetings or delete Google’s copies. Contact me to request access, corrections or deletion of meeting data. Your Google Calendar invitation also identifies the meeting.

External services

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, the 3D experience and other destinations have their own behavior and policies. The 3D experience uses essential session storage to preserve journey progress. The hosting provider may process ordinary access logs and IP addresses for delivery and security. Google Identity Services loads when you open the booking form. The owner’s calendar credentials are stored on the scheduling server.
